## Next Gen gates

- Entrant must be actively enrolled in high school, college, university, bootcamp, or another qualifying academic program.
- A qualifying student or academic email is required; RevenueCat may use domain-based verification.
- The entry needs a public open-source repository with source, assets, setup instructions, and a detectable license.
- The demo requires a public YouTube or Vimeo video under two minutes and code access.
- Next Gen does not require an App Store or Google Play release or paid developer account and may use RevenueCat Test Store.

## Submission assets

- English materials, or English translations.
- A 1024×1024 app icon.
- At least one 1179×2556 screenshot without a device frame.
- Device/platform footage in the public demo video.
- No unauthorized copyrighted music, footage, or third-party marks.

## Next Gen judging

Judges assess whether the idea is clear, useful, interesting, and original; whether meaningful working progress is visible in the video and code; whether RevenueCat is used thoughtfully; and whether technical/product choices show care.
